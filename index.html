About the firm

Independent, and built on real experience.

Borealis Investments is an independent, privately held investment firm based in Dallas, Texas, led by Jarrid and Norm Franke.

Between them, the partners bring decades of hands-on experience — Jarrid across the energy industry and engineering leadership, and Norm across more than four decades of community banking. That combination shapes a disciplined, long-term, fundamentals-driven approach.

We believe in being straightforward about who we are. We'll share full details on the firm, its structure, and any applicable disclosures directly with prospective partners.

Leadership

The people behind the firm.

JF

Jarrid Franke

Founder & Managing Partner

Based in Dallas, Jarrid leads Borealis Investments, setting the firm's investment strategy and direction. He brings 17 years in the energy industry — eight of them leading multidisciplinary teams — and currently serves as VP of Engineering at EQV, following roles at Stephens Natural Resources, Ovintiv, and Newfield Exploration. A petroleum engineer by training, he pairs deep technical and data discipline with a long-term, fundamentals-driven approach to capital.

VP of Engineering, EQVB.S. Petroleum Eng., Colorado School of Mines
NF

Norm Franke

Co-Founder & Partner

A career Colorado banker and Fort Lewis College graduate, Norm began at Alpine Bank in 1983 as a management trainee and became a Regional President in 1990. In 1991 he opened the bank's first Mesa County location, growing its regional presence over the following two decades before leading its expansion across the Front Range, including flagship Denver-area branches. He brings decades of community-banking and relationship experience to the firm.
