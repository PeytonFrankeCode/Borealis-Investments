About the firm

Independent, fiduciary, and accountable.

Borealis Investments is an independent registered investment adviser headquartered in Dallas, Texas. We act as a fiduciary, which means we are legally obligated to put our clients' interests first.

The firm was founded in 2014 and works with a focused group of client households and institutions. We are not affiliated with any broker-dealer, and client assets are held by an independent, third-party qualified custodian — never by the firm directly.

Our disclosures, registration status, and Form ADV are publicly available and can be verified through the regulators listed below.
